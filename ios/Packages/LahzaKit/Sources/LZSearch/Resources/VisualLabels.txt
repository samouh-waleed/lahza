# Words for what's in photos, mapped to Vision's photo tags (VisionLabels.txt). Drafted by hand; every tag must be one
# of Vision's identifiers (a test checks). One concept per line:
#
#   concept | words | tags | related tags | kind | person
#
# concept       the name chips and reasons use ("hiking")
# words         what people type, every form ("hike, hikes, hiked, trek"); phrases of up to 3 words
# tags          searched strictly: a photo with one of them can be Best
# related tags  searched loosely: a photo with only these goes under More (decision E24), and can be removed
#               specific ones only: a broad tag (sky, hill, path, night sky, a body of water, document, sport) is on
#               so many photos of a real library that it fills More
# kind          occasion (something you do: the outing counts), thing (something you see), document (a paper or a card)
# person        yes when the tag means people doing it (scored lower without a person, later)
#
# Every other Vision tag is searchable by its own name ("sand dune" for sand_dune, plurals too) without a line here.
# Words with no tag ("party", "gym", "selfie" as content) stay "not yet" until the image model lands.
