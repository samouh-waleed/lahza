Changes made for Lahza: historical, abandoned and destroyed places (PPLH, PPLQ, PPLW) are dropped; each place
keeps its name, position, population, feature class, country, region and time zone; English alternate names
are selected (places of 100,000+ people and capitals, up to 8 each) and curated English names are added;
everything is re-encoded in Lahza's binary place-list format.

The data is provided as is, without warranties of any kind.
